Interactive car. Drag or use arrow keys to orbit. Pinch, scroll, or use brackets to zoom. Tap doors to open them, lights to switch them on, or wheels to spin them. Keyboard shortcuts: O for doors, L for lights, D for wheels, R to reset, T to replay the tour.

This drawing needs WebGL2 and network access to load three.js from the CDN.
